1. Who we are and what this policy covers

OneHisab is a business management application developed by Md Raqibul Islam Masum under the MasumTech brand in Dhaka, Bangladesh. This policy describes information processed through OneHisab and its connected backend services. It does not replace the privacy policies of other MasumTech applications.

Some features depend on the app version, business configuration and enabled integrations. Descriptions of optional services apply when those services are used.

2. Information processed

Business users should enter and share customer, supplier and staff information only when authorised to do so. Avoid submitting passwords, access tokens or unrelated sensitive information in messages, support requests or AI inputs.

3. How information is used

  • Provide accounting, inventory, invoices, business reports and saved records.
  • Authenticate users and support configured cloud backup and synchronisation.
  • Deliver business communications requested or configured by authorised users.
  • Provide optional AI assistance and extract information from submitted invoices.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests, investigate failures and help prevent misuse.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information is disclosed to service providers as needed for enabled features, to recipients chosen by users, and to authorised people with access to the relevant business. Provider processing also depends on the applicable service terms and account configuration.

  • Google / Firebase: authentication and configured cloud storage, backup and synchronisation.
  • Meta / WhatsApp: WhatsApp business messaging, account identifiers, message content and delivery events.
  • Infobip: phone numbers, SMS content and delivery information when its SMS integration is used.
  • Google Gemini: submitted questions, invoice input and relevant information included in enabled AI requests.
  • Hosting and network providers, including Cloudflare where used: request traffic and connection information required to deliver and protect the backend.

When you export or share an invoice, PDF or message, the chosen recipient and application receive that content. Relevant information may also be disclosed where required by an applicable legal process or necessary to investigate misuse and protect affected users.

Provider information: Google privacy · Firebase privacy · WhatsApp privacy · Infobip privacy · Cloudflare privacy.

5. WhatsApp webhook records

The OneHisab gateway can receive and store WhatsApp event payloads, including message information and delivery statuses. These records support processing, troubleshooting and duplicate detection. A message reaching the gateway is not, by itself, an instruction to change accounting records.

6. Device access and local copies

Features involving invoice images, attachments, exports or notifications may require operating-system access or user selection. You can manage available permissions in device settings; restricting access may limit the corresponding feature. Exported files and data shared with other apps remain under the control of the devices and recipients holding those copies.

7. Storage, retention and security

Information may be stored on your device, in configured cloud services and in backend storage. Service providers may process information outside Bangladesh. Security depends on access controls, the service configuration and protection of user accounts and devices; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Business records are retained to provide the service and maintain the records requested by the business, subject to deletion requests. Technical and webhook records are retained for operational, security and troubleshooting needs. Any records that must remain for an applicable obligation or an unresolved dispute are limited to that purpose. Contact us for retention information relating to your account or a specific record.

Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete cloud or backend records. Backups, exported files and recipients' copies may require separate handling.

8. Access, correction and data deletion requests

You can ask about information associated with you, request correction, or request deletion of your OneHisab account or specified data.

  1. Email masumtech.dev@gmail.com with the subject OneHisab Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email or phone number associated with the account or WhatsApp conversation, your business name if relevant, and whether you want account deletion or deletion of particular records. Do not send a password, OTP or access token.
  3. We will verify the requester's identity and authority before deleting or disclosing information. Requests about a customer's records may require coordination with the business that created those records.
  4. We will explain the scope and expected timing, carry out the applicable deletion, and identify any information that must be retained and why. Where third-party or backup copies are involved, we will explain the available process and its limitations.
